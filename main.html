Enable Location in Windows & Chrome

                

                    Google Chrome cannot access your coordinates because Location Services are disabled in Windows Settings or blocked in browser permissions.
                

                

                    

                        
1

                        

                            Windows: "Let Windows and apps access your location"
                            Click the button below to open Windows Settings. Turn ON "Location services" and "Let desktop apps access your location".
                        

                    

                    

                        
2

                        

                            Chrome: Site Location Permission
                            Click the site settings / sliders icon on the left side of Chrome's address bar, and set Location to Allow.
                        

                    

                    

                        
3

                        

                            Windows Service: "Geolocation Service" (lfsvc)
                            If Location is already ON in Settings: Windows "Geolocation Service" (lfsvc) may be stopped/disabled. Run fix-windows-location.bat in the project folder, or press Win+R → services.msc → set Geolocation Service to Manual and click Start.
